# Welcome to our new Blog! 👋

We are incredibly excited to launch the **NITRR Open Source Community Blog**. This space is dedicated to celebrating the amazing work of our student developers, highlighting technical deep-dives into our open-source projects, and sharing important community updates.

## What to expect

Over the coming months, you can expect to see:

1. **Developer Spotlights:** Interviews and highlights featuring the top contributors from our Leaderboard.
2. **Project Showcases:** Deep dives into the technical challenges we face and how we overcome them.
3. **Event Recaps:** Summaries of our hackathons, open-source contribution drives (like Hacktoberfest), and community meetups.

### Want to write an article?

Are you a member of the community with something interesting to share? We'd love to feature your writing! You can submit an article by opening a Pull Request in our repository and adding a new Markdown file to the `src/content/blog` folder.
